## 🎯 The Secret to Passing Mercor, Micro1 & AI Interviewers

Automated AI interviewers (like Mercor and Micro1) use speech-to-text models paired with large language model evaluation rubrics. Candidates typically fail because:
1. **Giving 1-2 sentence shallow definitions**: The AI rubric flags this as "Lacks depth" or "Junior response."
2. **Rambling without technical keywords**: The AI evaluation looks for specific mechanical concepts (e.g., `ob_refcnt`, `epoll`, `HNSW`, `Cross-Encoder`, `CoW`, `AST`).
3. **Missing production trade-offs**: Senior engineers don't just explain how a feature works; they explain where it breaks and when NOT to use it.

### ⏱️ The 4-Part 60-Second Answer Formula:
* **Seconds 0 – 15 (The Direct Hook & Mental Model)**: Give the exact architectural definition immediately without filler phrases.
* **Seconds 15 – 45 (Under-the-Hood Mechanics)**: Explain how CPython, the OS kernel, or the vector engine handles this in memory.
* **Seconds 45 – 70 (Production Trade-offs & Scale)**: Share real-world production gotchas, failure modes under load, or concurrency risks.
* **Seconds 70 – 90 (Concrete Implementation)**: Mention how you implement this in code (e.g., using `TaskGroup`, `pydantic-settings`, or `asyncpg`).
